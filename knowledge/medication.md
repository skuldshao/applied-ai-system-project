# Medication timing notes

General notes on *when* to schedule medicine, for any species. They never give doses: amounts
always come from the pet's vet, and PawPal+ refuses dose questions before they reach the model.

## Giving medicine on time
<!-- species: any | stage: any | keywords: medicine, medication, meds, pill, tablet, twice, daily -->
Give medicine at the same times every day, exactly as the vet prescribed. Twice-daily medicine
is usually given about 12 hours apart, for example at 08:00 and 20:00.

## Insulin for diabetic pets
<!-- species: any | stage: any | keywords: insulin, diabetes, diabetic, injection, shot -->
Diabetic pets usually get insulin right after a meal, so the owner knows the pet has eaten
first. Schedule each insulin task to start as soon as a meal ends, about 12 hours apart. Never
change an insulin dose without the vet.

## Eye and ear drops
<!-- species: any | stage: any | keywords: drops, eye, ear -->
If a pet gets more than one kind of drop, wait about 5 minutes between them so the second does
not wash out the first. Drops are often prescribed two to four times a day.

## Pills with food
<!-- species: any | stage: any | keywords: pill, tablet, food, meal, breakfast, dinner -->
Many pills are easier to give with or right after food. Only give a pill with food if the label
or the vet says that is fine.
