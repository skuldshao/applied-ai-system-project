# Cat care notes

General care notes that the PawPal+ AI Planner retrieves before planning. Each `##` section is
one note; the comment under its heading tags who it applies to. These notes are general
guidance, not veterinary advice: the pet's vet always comes first.

## Feeding cats
<!-- species: cat | stage: any | keywords: feed, meal, breakfast, dinner, food -->
Adult cats do best with two or more meals a day at regular times. Kittens need three to four
small meals a day.

## Play for cats
<!-- species: cat | stage: any | keywords: play, toy, exercise, hunt -->
Cats hunt in short bursts, so two play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes a day work better than one
long session. Playing in the evening, before the last meal, helps a cat settle for the night.

## Litter box
<!-- species: cat | stage: any | keywords: litter, box, scoop, clean -->
Scoop the litter box every day; many cats avoid a dirty box. Schedule scooping as a daily task,
and change the litter completely about once a week.

## Grooming adult cats
<!-- species: cat | stage: adult | keywords: brush, groom, fur, hair -->
Short-haired adult cats need brushing about once a week; long-haired cats need it most days.

## Senior cats
<!-- species: cat | stage: senior | keywords: senior, old, brush, groom, feed, play -->
Cats aged 11 and over groom themselves less, so gentle brushing a few times a week helps. Keep
meals at the same times every day, and keep play sessions short and gentle.
