# Dog care notes

General care notes that the PawPal+ AI Planner retrieves before planning. Each `##` section is
one note; the comment under its heading tags who it applies to. These notes are general
guidance, not veterinary advice: the pet's vet always comes first.

## Walks for puppies
<!-- species: dog | stage: young | keywords: walk, exercise, puppy -->
Puppies under a year old tire quickly and their joints are still growing. Plan several short
walks of about 15 minutes spread through the day instead of one long walk.

## Walks for adult dogs
<!-- species: dog | stage: adult | keywords: walk, exercise -->
Healthy adult dogs do well with a 30-minute walk, once or twice a day. Morning and
early-evening walks fit most routines.

## Walks for senior dogs
<!-- species: dog | stage: senior | keywords: walk, exercise, senior, old -->
Dogs aged 8 and over often have stiff joints and tire sooner. Keep each walk gentle and short,
about 20 minutes, and prefer two short walks (morning and evening) to one long one.

## Exercise after meals
<!-- species: dog | stage: any | keywords: walk, exercise, play, fetch, meal, breakfast, dinner, feed -->
Wait about an hour after a meal before a walk or active play. Exercise on a full stomach raises
the risk of bloat, an emergency, especially in large, deep-chested dogs.

## Walks in hot weather
<!-- species: dog | stage: any | keywords: walk, afternoon, midday, noon, heat, hot, summer -->
Avoid walks between 12:00 and 16:00 on warm days, when pavements are hottest and dogs overheat
quickly. Early-morning and evening walks are safer.

## Feeding dogs
<!-- species: dog | stage: any | keywords: feed, meal, breakfast, dinner, food -->
Most adult dogs eat two meals a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart, at the same times every day.
Puppies usually need three or four smaller meals.
